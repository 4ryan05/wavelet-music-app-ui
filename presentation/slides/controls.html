This maps our screens to Module 4, screen-based controls. Operable controls: buttons. Text entry: text boxes. Selection controls: radio buttons for one-of-many, checkboxes for any-of-many, and toggles for instant on or off. Presentation and custom controls: the seek slider and filter chips. Each is a Figma component with its states, such as default, focused, error and disabled.
