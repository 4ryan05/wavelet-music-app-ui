Wavelet · Music Streaming App UI

9

Modern tool usage in Figma: colors, spacing and corner radius are Figma variables, and typography uses 9 Inter text styles. On top of that we built 16 components with variants, for example Button with Primary, Secondary and Ghost, and a Track Row with a Playing state, plus 35 icons. Every screen is made of instances of these, so one change updates the whole app. Dark theme with one violet accent; text contrast is at least 4.5 to 1.
