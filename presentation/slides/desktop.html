Module 5’s self-learning topic: designing for desktop, laptop and large screens. With more space, the bottom tab bar becomes a sidebar that also lists your playlists, the mini player grows into a full player bar with volume and device controls, and Now Playing becomes a side panel with lyrics and the queue. The components and tokens are the same as on mobile.
