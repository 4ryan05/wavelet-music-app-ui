Search puts the search bar at the top and offers a genre grid for people who don’t have an exact query: recognition over recall. Now Playing uses large artwork, a clear title, a seek slider and a 72 px play button placed where the thumb rests. Up Next uses a segmented control to switch between the queue, lyrics and related songs; the playing song is highlighted with an equalizer icon, which gives system-status feedback.
