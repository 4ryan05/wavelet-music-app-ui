Know your users

Two personas guided every decision

Riya, 20

Engineering student · listens on the bus

Needs · quick focus playlists, offline downloads to save data, lyrics to sing along

Frustrations · busy home screens, interruptions during study sessions

Device · Android phone, one-handed use

Prakash, 45

Bank manager · casual listener

Needs · large, clear controls and simple search for old Kannada and Hindi classics

Frustrations · tiny icons with no labels, confusing settings

Device · phone at home, laptop at work

Wavelet · Music Streaming App UI

4

Module 2, know your client: we defined two personas with very different needs. Riya is young, mobile-first and wants focus music and offline downloads. Prakash wants big, clear, labelled controls and an easy way to find classics. Designing for both pushed us towards large touch targets, labels under every icon and a simple navigation structure.
