UI design principles · Module 1

Six principles we designed by

Consistency

Shared tokens and components: the same tab bar, buttons and spacing everywhere.

System status

Mini player, progress bar and the “Playing” state always show what is happening.

Recognition over recall

Every navigation icon has a text label; genres let people browse.

Fitts’s law

52 px buttons and a 72 px play button, placed in the thumb zone.

Error prevention

Inline form validation; Log out kept apart at the bottom of Settings.

Accessibility

Text contrast of at least 4.5 : 1 and touch targets of at least 44 px.
