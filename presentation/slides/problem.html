The problem

Music apps make you work too hard just to press play

Cluttered home screens

Ads, banners and endless shelves compete for attention.

Icons without labels

Users have to guess what a symbol means, especially less tech-savvy listeners.

Too many taps

Finding a song or resuming playback takes several steps.

Our goal: any song within 3 taps, and playback always 1 tap away, on mobile and desktop.
